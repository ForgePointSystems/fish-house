# Glossary

Domain terms, in the facility's own words. Where our term and theirs differ,
theirs wins.

**2026-08-11:** terms below marked *(confirmed)* come from reading
`inventory-2026-08-03.xlsx` and `order-dispatch-doc-2026-08-03.docx` directly —
see `docs/reference/sheet-findings.md` for full detail and examples. Everything
else is still a placeholder to confirm with the owner.

## Core entities

**Lot** *(confirmed, finfish)* — a specific catch, identified by a **Histamine
Lot Code** in the format `{SOURCE}-{SPECIES}-{MMDDYY}`, e.g. `PDL-ALM-080326`
(Peddler dock, Almaco jack, landed 8/3/26). One row in the `Inventory` tab is
one lot. "Histamine" in the column name is a food-safety term specific to
scombroid species (tuna and related fish) — worth confirming whether the code
is used for all species or only histamine-risk ones.

**Lot** *(confirmed, shellfish)* — shellfish use a different, regulatory
format: a harvest-area letter+digit code plus a date, e.g. `H5 8/1`, `D3 8/2`.
This is very likely the NC molluscan shellfish harvest tag required by law —
worth confirming, since if so it must be preserved verbatim, not reformatted.

**Pull** — removing product from inventory against an order. Still to confirm
this is the facility's own word for it; not seen verbatim in the documents.

**Order** *(confirmed, informal)* — a line in the weekly dispatch doc:
restaurant name, an invoice number, and free-text quantities, e.g. `STANBURY
66439 inv: 2 gal OY, 4 LUMP, 100 CROATAN D3 8/2`. Not a structured record
anywhere today — see open question 2 and 11.

**Invoice number** *(confirmed)* — a 5-digit sequential number (e.g. `66439`)
attached to most wholesale order lines. Likely sourced from an external
accounting system (QuickBooks or similar), not generated by hand. Worth
confirming the source before assuming the app can safely mint its own.

**Route / driver** *(confirmed)* — each delivery day is split into named
routes, each with an assigned driver and sometimes a specific vehicle, e.g.
"THU CENTRAL ROUTE - MIKE", "drive MB3 RALEIGH/CARY". Not modeled in the
current design and out of scope for v1's chosen channel (see question 11).

**EOD (end of day) count** *(confirmed)* — a nightly hand-count typed as a flat
list at the close of each day's section, e.g. `93 mbl 8/6-ON`, `245 LSH`. This
is the physical count the rollout plan's phase-3 cutover attaches to.

**Yield** — product out over product in across a processing step. The `FF LOG`
tab computes this explicitly (`True Yield`, start weight, finish weight), so
it's tracked today, at least for some product lines. Granularity per pull vs.
per batch not yet confirmed.

## Codes seen in the documents (partial, unconfirmed meanings)

Cut/treatment suffixes on species codes: `on` = skin-on, `off` = skin/skinless,
`sg` = likely scaled-and-gutted, `loin` = loin cut, `dry` = dry-packed,
`IQF` = individually quick frozen, `PD` = peeled and deveined (shrimp).

Species abbreviations appear throughout (`LBTILE`, `MBTILE`, `JGTILE`, `LSH`,
`ARS`, `AROCK`, `GAG`, `BET`, `CAT`, `LF`...) — a full code-to-species mapping
should come directly from the owner rather than being guessed at, since a wrong
guess baked into the schema is expensive to unwind.

## Confirmed business shape

- Multiple sales channels: wholesale restaurant accounts, retail chain accounts
  with formal PO numbers (Whole Foods), a "shares" CSA-style subscription
  program, farmers markets, and out-of-state shipping.
- Multiple product categories: whole/fillet finfish, live oysters and clams,
  live crabs, live crawfish, frozen and smoked goods, dry goods.
- Possibly multiple physical locations — `Wanchese Frozen Inv` is a separate
  tab from the main `Inventory` tab (see open question 13).
- A satellite Asheville/WNC operation with its own van route, distinct from
  the main Raleigh-area routes.
